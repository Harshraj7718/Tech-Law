Case 01UK · IN · FintechPre-Series A

How a UK–India fintech startup protected its proprietary algorithms prior to Series A.

Challenge

Core risk-scoring algorithms were written by contractors in two countries with no assignment terms — a red flag in investor due diligence.

Strategy

Confirmatory IP assignments under Indian and English law, a trade-secret protocol for the model, and Class 9 & 36 filings via Madrid.

Outcome

Clean chain of title delivered inside the diligence window. The round closed on schedule.

Case 02IN · Consumer goodsPost-launch

How a D2C skincare brand took its name back from marketplace copycats.

Challenge

Lookalike listings using the brand’s mark appeared across three marketplaces within weeks of launch.

Strategy

Expedited registration, coordinated platform takedowns and a cease-and-desist programme against repeat sellers.

Outcome

Infringing listings removed. A watch service now flags conflicting filings before they publish.

Case 03UK · Software & AILicensing deal

How a generative-AI studio clarified who owns its model’s output before a UK licensing deal.

Challenge

A UK licensee required ownership warranties on AI-generated assets that the studio could not yet give.

Strategy

Mapped human authorship in the pipeline against CDPA s.9(3), audited training-data licences and restructured the warranty package.

Outcome

Warranties the studio could stand behind, and a licence signed without price concessions.
