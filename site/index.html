§ 04  Matters · anonymised

Resolved.

Client details are withheld. Each matter sets out the problem, the strategy and what it protected.

Matter 01UK · IN · FintechPre-Series A

How a UK–India fintech secured its proprietary algorithms before Series A.

Challenge

Core risk-scoring code was written by contractors in two countries with no assignment terms — a red flag in investor due diligence.

Strategy

Confirmatory IP assignments under Indian and English law, a trade-secret protocol for the model, and Class 9 and 36 filings through Madrid.

Outcome

A clean chain of title delivered inside the diligence window. The round closed on schedule.

Matter 02IN · Consumer goodsPost-launch

How a D2C skincare brand took its name back from marketplace copycats.

Challenge

Lookalike listings using the brand’s mark appeared across three marketplaces within weeks of launch.

Strategy

Expedited registration, coordinated platform takedowns and a cease-and-desist programme against repeat sellers.

Outcome

Infringing listings removed, and a watch service that now flags conflicting filings before they publish.

Matter 03UK · Software & AILicensing deal

How a generative-AI studio clarified who owns its model’s output before a UK licensing deal.

Challenge

A UK licensee wanted ownership warranties over AI-generated assets that the studio could not yet give.

Strategy

Mapped human authorship in the pipeline against s.9(3) CDPA, audited training-data licences and restructured the warranty package.

Outcome

Warranties the studio could stand behind, and a licence signed without price concessions.
